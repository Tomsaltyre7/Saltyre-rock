SALTYRE ROCK

The Island of Saltyre Rock is a dangerous place full of rebels and sea pirates. One wrong step or one word of disrespect will put you down for good. The King of Saltyre is a sinful and wicked man who takes pleasure in tormenting his subjects, King William the mad. What makes all this of the King worse is the peasants whisper, rumors abound with such accusations as he is a sorcerer, indulges in cannibal orgies of human meat eating, commits violence and drinks the blood of his servants.
